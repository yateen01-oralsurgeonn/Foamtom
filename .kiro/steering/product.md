# Product: Foamtom: The Haunted Mirror

## What it is
A free browser game that turns a phone or tablet propped by the bathroom sink into a
haunted mirror. The child sees their own reflection in a cursed-mirror frame (candle
flicker, fog, cracked glass). Cute-spooky cavity monsters cling to the cheek over the
part of the mouth that still needs brushing. Brushing that part makes the monster
shriek and dissolve into smoke. A full brush is 2 minutes.

## Who it is for
- **Player:** children aged 5-9 who brush alone at home. They read little, so every
  instruction is spoken aloud and shown as an icon or animation, never only as text.
- **Parent:** sees a small summary screen (time brushed, which zone is most often missed).
- **Author:** Dr. Yatin Shinde, a practising dentist in India. The dental rules in
  `dental-rules.md` are his and are not to be changed without his say.

## The problem
Children of this age stop brushing early and skip the same areas, often the back teeth.
A timer alone does not show them *where* they skipped.

## Core loop
1. Open a web link. No install, no sign-up, no account.
2. Allow the front camera, or skip it (guided-timer mode).
3. Four zones, 30 seconds each. The voice names the zone ("A ghoul is hiding upper left!").
   A monster sits over that zone.
4. Brushing the right zone shrinks the monster to smoke. Brushing the wrong side does nothing.
5. At 2:00 the trapped monsters go into the **Monster Book**.

## Why the child comes back
- **Monster Book:** a collection of 10-12 monster designs. Rare ones appear only when the
  child brushes both morning and night on the same day.
- **Parent screen:** total brushing time and the zone most often missed.

## Tone
Cute-spooky, never scary. No gore, no jump scares, no sudden loud sounds. A 5-year-old
should giggle, not cry. Monsters are defeated, not hurt.
